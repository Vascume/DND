Suspended between two cliffs, the Ridgeway Span is a latticework bridge of steel and cedar that sways gently with the mountain winds. Lanterns of green flame line its spine, casting eerie light across the mist, and the creak of its timbers is said to sound like a giant’s harp. Crossing the Span is both a rite of passage and a test of nerve—spelunkers and traders alike must trust the bridge’s strength as they step into the void, the valley yawning beneath their feet.
