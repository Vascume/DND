A dwarven-led assembly of guildmasters, miners, and smiths who oversee the practical needs of the city—labor, safety in the Undercity, and the maintenance of the suspension bridges. They still honor dwarven traditions and laws, especially around the [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Deepmouth Gate]].

Led by [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Borin Deepmantle]]
