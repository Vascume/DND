_Westmore: The Backbone of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]_

Westmore is the grit beneath [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s gleam—the city’s steadfast, hammer-hearted half. Clinging to the western mountain spine, it hums with the rhythm of labor and legacy. While Eascros may dazzle with trade and scholarship, Westmore endures with calloused hands, roaring furnaces, and the quiet pride of those who shape the world from stone and steel.

 The [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Anvil Ledge]]

A broad terrace hammered flat against the cliffside, the **[[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Anvil Ledge]]** is the beating heart of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s craft. Here, smiths temper blades, mend armor, and forge tools for the Empire’s expeditions. Miners gather to test gear before descending into the depths, their laughter mingling with the hiss of quenching steel. Sparks drift into the valley like red snow, and the clang of hammers echoes across the ridge—a sound so constant it’s become the city’s unofficial anthem.

Travelers often pause here, drawn by the spectacle. Some say the mountain itself sings with iron, its voice carried on the wind. Others swear the rhythm of the forge is a kind of magic, older than the Empire, older even than the dwarves who first carved this place from the stone.

 The [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Deepmouth Gate]]

Set into the cliff like the maw of a slumbering giant, the **[[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Deepmouth Gate]]** is the original dwarven entrance into the Undercity. Its massive stone ribs are carved with runes that record the names of caverns like ancestral lineages—each glyph a memory, each groove a vow. Though the banners of the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]] now hang above it, the Gate remains sacred. Miners touch the stone before entering, whispering oaths to the Serpent Below for safe passage.

Beyond the threshold lies a labyrinth of cool halls, ancient archives, and hidden vaults. Dwarven law still holds sway here, even under imperial oversight. The Undercity is a place of secrets and stone, where the past is etched into every wall and the future is mined one swing at a time.

_Taverns of Westmore_

[[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/The Emberjaw]]

Nestled just off the [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Anvil Ledge]], [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/The Emberjaw]] is a favorite haunt of smiths, miners, and adventurers fresh from the Undercity. Its stone hearth burns day and night, fed by enchanted coals that never cool. The walls are lined with cracked helmets, bent pickaxes, and signed contracts—testaments to jobs completed and lives risked.

The ale is strong, the food is hearty, and the barkeep, a retired dwarven delver named [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Grunna Emberjaw]], keeps a strict code: no weapons drawn, no lies told, and no debts unpaid. Rumors often start here, and so do expeditions. If you want a lead on a lost vault or a cursed vein of ore, this is where you listen.

_Signature Drink:_ “Molten Mercy” — a smoky stout served hot, with a dash of fireweed.

[[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/The Hanging Pick]]

Perched near the [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Deepmouth Gate]], [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/The Hanging Pick]] caters to those about to descend—or those lucky enough to return. Its name comes from the rusted mining pick that swings above the door, said to have belonged to the first dwarf who breached the Undercity’s lower vaults.

Inside, the tavern is dim and cool, with lanterns made from old helmet visors and tables carved from discarded mine carts. The clientele is quieter, more superstitious. Here, miners share tales of strange echoes, glowing veins, and things that move in the dark. Imperial agents sometimes linger in the corners, listening.

_Signature Drink:_ “Serpent’s Breath” — a chilled, bitter brew laced with mountain mint and a hint of silver dust.
