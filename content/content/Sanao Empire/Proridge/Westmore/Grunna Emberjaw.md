Grunna Emberjaw is a retired dwarven delver and the no-nonsense barkeep of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/The Emberjaw]] tavern. Known for her ironclad rules and sharper wit, she keeps order among miners and adventurers with the same grit she once used in the Undercity.
