A cornerstone of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Westmore]]’s rugged charm, the Emberjaw is a forge-warmed tavern where the scent of coal smoke clings to every beam. Frequented by smiths, miners, and adventurers fresh from the Undercity, it’s a place where contracts are struck over mugs of molten-dark stout and tales are traded like currency. The barkeep, [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Grunna Emberjaw]], enforces a strict code of honesty and order, and her reputation ensures that even the rowdiest mercenaries mind their manners—most of the time.
