The Deepmouth Gate is the original dwarven entrance into the Undercity, its massive stone ribs carved with runes that record the names of caverns like ancestral lineages. Though the Empire’s banners now hang above it, the Gate remains a sacred threshold—miners touch the stone before entering, whispering oaths to the Serpent Below for safe passage. Beyond lies a labyrinth of cool halls, archives, and hidden vaults, where dwarven law still holds sway even under imperial oversight.
