Shadowed by the [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Deepmouth Gate]], the Hanging Pick is a quieter, more superstitious haunt. Its dim interior is lit by lanterns fashioned from old mining gear, and the air carries the scent of damp stone and bitter brew. Here, miners toast to safe returns and whisper of strange happenings in the deep. The tavern’s namesake—a rusted pick suspended above the hearth—serves as both a relic and a warning: the mountain gives, but it also remembers.
