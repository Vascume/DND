A broad-shouldered dwarf with a beard streaked in iron-grey, Borin Deepmantle is the most respected voice on [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/The Stone Council]]. Once a master miner who mapped the Undercity’s deepest shafts, Borin now serves as guardian of dwarven tradition. He is pragmatic, stubborn, and fiercely protective of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Westmore]]’s autonomy. Though he tolerates the Empire’s presence, he believes the mountain belongs to its people first, and he often clashes with imperial officials over mining rights and cultural preservation. Adventurers may find him a stern ally—if they prove their respect for dwarven ways
