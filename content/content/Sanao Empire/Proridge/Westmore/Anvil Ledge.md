A broad terrace hammered flat against the cliffside, the Anvil Ledge is the beating heart of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s craft. Here, smiths temper tools and miners test their gear before descending into the shafts. Sparks drift into the valley like red snow, and the rhythmic clang of hammers echoes across the ridge, a sound so constant it has become the city’s unofficial anthem. Travelers often pause here to watch the spectacle, marveling at how the mountain itself seems to sing with iron.
