## Proridge within the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]]

_The wind howls like a living thing through the Ironspine Mountains, tugging at cloaks and rattling the chains of the great suspension bridges that stretch across the yawning ravine. Far below, mist churns like a restless sea, hiding whatever secrets the deep stone keeps._

_Above it all, carved into the twin spines of the mountains, sprawls the city of Proridge._

To the west: [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Westmore]]—a city of soot-stained stone and calloused hands. Blacksmiths hammer through the night, miners descend into the depths, and taverns echo with laughter, brawls, and tales of coin earned the hard way.

To the east: [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Eascros]]—a city of marble towers and arcane light. Scholars debate in vaulted halls, artists sketch the skyline from balconies, and merchants haggle over goods from across the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]].

Bridges of steel and spellwork bind the two halves, swaying gently in the mountain breeze. They are lifelines, symbols of unity—and sometimes, of tension.

Proridge is no longer just a dwarven city. It is a beacon of opportunity, a staging ground for adventurers, and a jewel in the crown of the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]]. Here, fortunes are made, secrets are unearthed, and legends are born.

You arrive as the sun dips behind the peaks, casting long shadows across the stone. Somewhere in the city, a bell tolls. A new contract is posted. A door creaks open. And your story begins…
## Origins in stone and empire

The first [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]] was a sanctum: vaults and forges chiseled into basalt, chambers measured not in steps but in echoes. When the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]] claimed the range, the city stepped out onto terraces and ropeways, binding ridge to ridge with steel and cedar, marrying dwarven craft to imperial logistics. Old runes still guard the [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Deepmouth Gate]], but a silvered imperial sigil sits above them, a reminder that even mountains can be annexed.

## Life and trade under imperial reach

Morning begins with winch-songs and the grind of pit-carts, a rhythm that keeps teams in time from surface quarries to deep seams. Traders carry ore and cold-burning lamp-oils down to the Empire’s heartlands, while imperial factors send back rope, steel, and paper seals that turn tunnels into revenue streams. Market Wardens in green-lacquered pauldrons keep blood off the stones; their jurisdiction ends at the Deepmouth—beyond that, dwarven law and stone oaths prevail.

## Culture of miners and spelunkers

- Heirloom tools: Picks and lamps carry etched depth-marks and cave-names, passed through kin like sagas you can hold.
    
- Maps as music: Cave charts are inked on slate and sung as ballads; a good map can win you a permit faster than a bribe.
    
- Trial of the Drop: New climbers free-hang the training shaft to learn trust—in knots, lines, and silence.
    
- [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Twin Serpents]] rites: Lantern vigils mark new shafts; two green flames set side by side until the stone “accepts” the work.
    

## Friction and whispers

- **Slipglass veins:** **A beautiful, deadly slick**; Wardens dust it with grit, dwarves mark it with blue chalk, and the Empire levies fines for ignoring either.
    
- **The Hollow Choir:** **Echoing caverns** where sound returns in layered harmony; veterans say the echoes remember names and warn of collapses.
    
- **Lost bell nights:** **Old shaft bells** ring without hands when fog crawls the ridge, as if the mountain is auditing its debts.
    
- **Sealed vault:** **A rune-locked door** in the Undercity rumored to hold a map of an “under-ocean”; imperial scholars petition yearly to open it and are refused.
    

## Texture at the edge

At dusk, blue-white lanterns bead the terraces and the Empire’s green flames thread the spans, two lights twinned like the city’s loyalties. Faces return dusted with mica and quiet pride, contracts are thumb-marked against crystal, and the ridge answers the wind with the soft clatter of gear. [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]] keeps time with the mountain, but it hums with imperial ambition—stone-sure, sky-facing, and never fully tamed.
