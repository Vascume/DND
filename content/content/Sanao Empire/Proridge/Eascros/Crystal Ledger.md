Carved into the cliff face, the Crystal Ledger is a hall whose walls glitter with embedded geodes. Here, miners register their finds, claims, and debts, etching records onto crystal tablets that hum faintly when touched. The Ledger is both archive and shrine, a place where the mountain’s treasures are catalogued and disputes settled under the watchful eyes of imperial clerks. Locals believe the crystals themselves remember every transaction, binding promises more tightly than parchment ever could.
