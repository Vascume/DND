An imperial-appointed office headquartered in the [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Imperial Tallyhouse]]. The Prefect and their clerks manage trade, taxation, and scholarly institutions, ensuring [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s wealth flows back to the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]]. They wield bureaucratic power rather than military might, but their influence is undeniable.
