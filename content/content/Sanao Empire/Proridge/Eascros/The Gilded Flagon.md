In the polished heart of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Eascros]], the Gilded Flagon offers refinement without pretense. Its quartz-inlaid walls and magically tuned harp create an atmosphere of quiet luxury, where scholars sip enchanted cocktails and merchants negotiate over velvet-draped tables. The staff are discreet, the drinks are potent, and the rumors that pass through its doors are often worth more than the wine. For those who deal in secrets and silver, the Flagon is a second home.
