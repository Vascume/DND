_Eascros: The Gilded Ascent_

Where [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Westmore]] is forged in fire and stone, Eascros rises in light and learning. Clinging to the eastern spine of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]], this half of the city gleams with ambition. Marble terraces, glass-domed archives, and arcane towers stretch skyward, catching the sun as it crests the peaks. Here, the clang of hammers gives way to the rustle of parchment, the chime of coin, and the low hum of enchantments at work.

Eascros is the domain of merchants, scholars, and imperial agents. It is where fortunes are tallied, disputes are arbitrated, and the mountain’s wealth is transformed into influence. Though the dwarves of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Westmore/Westmore]] may grumble at its elegance, none deny that Eascros is where power gathers—and where it is spent.

 The [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Crystal Ledger]]

Carved directly into the cliff face, the [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Crystal Ledger]] is a hall of shimmering reverence. Its walls glitter with embedded geodes, each one pulsing faintly with inner light. Here, miners and prospectors register their finds, claims, and debts, etching their records onto crystal tablets that hum when touched—resonating with the magic of truth and memory.

The Ledger is both archive and shrine. Imperial clerks in silver-trimmed robes oversee the process, settling disputes with cold precision. Locals believe the crystals themselves remember every transaction, binding promises more tightly than any parchment ever could. Some whisper that the Ledger is alive, and that to lie within its walls is to invite the mountain’s wrath.

 The [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Imperial Tallyhouse]]

A squat, fortress-like structure of black stone and iron shutters, the **[[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Imperial Tallyhouse]]** is the bureaucratic heart of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]. Here, the Empire weighs and taxes the city’s bounty—ore, gems, lamp-oils, and more. Every shipment is logged, every permit for a new shaft stamped in ash-black wax, every discrepancy noted with chilling efficiency.

Though its presence is resented by many of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s older families, the Tallyhouse ensures order. Its clerks are trained in both arithmetic and arcana, and some say they can sense the worth of a gem before it’s even unearthed. To cross them is to risk more than fines—it is to risk imperial scrutiny, and that is a weight few can bear.

[[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Eascros]] is where the mountain’s riches are measured, its secrets studied, and its future shaped. It is a place of elegance and edge, where knowledge is power and every coin carries a shadow.


[[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/The Gilded Flagon]]

Tucked between a jeweler’s guildhall and a perfumer’s atelier, [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/The Gilded Flagon]] is where [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/Eascros]] unwinds in style. This upscale tavern caters to scholars, merchants, and off-duty imperial officers. Its walls are paneled in darkwood and inlaid with veins of quartz that shimmer in candlelight. A magical harp plays softly in the corner, adjusting its tune to match the mood of the room.

The Flagon is known for its rare vintages and experimental cocktails—many infused with minor enchantments that warm the blood, sharpen the mind, or, in one infamous case, briefly allow the drinker to speak fluent Draconic. Deals are struck here over velvet booths and whispered to servers who know better than to repeat what they hear.

_Signature Drink:_ “Imperial Whisper” — a silvery liqueur that leaves a faint echo of your last spoken word in the air.

[[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/The Prism Veil]]

Built into a natural amphitheater carved from crystal-veined stone, [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Eascros/The Prism Veil]] is Eascros’s premier theater and arcane performance hall. Its stage is enchanted to shift backdrops with illusion magic, and its acoustics are so finely tuned that even a whisper carries to the highest seats. The Veil hosts everything from classical dwarven tragedies to experimental illusion ballets and magical storytelling duels.

The theater is a cultural hub, drawing nobles, scholars, and artists from across the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]]. Performers are often sponsored by guilds or imperial patrons, and a successful debut here can launch a career—or end one in spectacular fashion.

_Current Production:_ _“Ash and Echoes”_ — a historical drama about the founding of [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]], blending illusion magic with live performance.
