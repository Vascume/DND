Liraen Veynar

Elegant and calculating, Liraen Veynar is a high elf appointed by the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]] to oversee [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s wealth and ensure its prosperity flows back to the imperial coffers. Clad in black and silver robes, Liraen is known for her sharp intellect and unyielding adherence to imperial law. She views [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]] as a vital frontier hub and seeks to expand its influence, often at the expense of dwarven traditions. Though outwardly courteous, she is ruthless in negotiation, and her clerks whisper that she knows the value of every vein of ore before it is mined. Adventurers may find her patronage lucrative—but dangerous.
