A squat, fortress-like hall built of black stone, the Imperial Tallyhouse is where the Empire weighs [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s wealth. Ore, gems, and lamp-oils are measured and taxed, with permits for new shafts stamped in ash-black wax. Its presence is a constant reminder that [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s prosperity feeds the wider [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]]. Though dwarves grumble at the bureaucracy, the Tallyhouse ensures order—and its clerks are rumored to know the value of every vein before the miners themselves.
