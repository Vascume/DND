Dominating the marketplace, the Serpents’ Statue depicts two colossal coils entwined, one gazing skyward, the other downward into the earth. Citizens gather here to light twin lanterns, invoking balance between ambition and secrecy. During festivals, masked dancers circle the statue in spirals of firelight, their movements said to mimic the endless coil of the guardians. The statue is both shrine and warning: a reminder that every deal struck in [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Gragos]] has consequences above and below.
