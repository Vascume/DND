Stretching along the Arathis River, the Iron Docks are a thunderous symphony of chains, barges, and shouting crews. Ore, timber, and contraband flow through its piers, feeding [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Gragos]]’s insatiable appetite for trade and intrigue. Sailors whisper of spirits that linger in the mist, demanding tribute from the unwary, and dockside taverns thrive on tales of fortune and ruin. The Empire’s green-flame lanterns burn here too, casting eerie light across the water, as if daring smugglers to test their luck.
