### Origins

- The Twin Serpents are said to be ancient guardians born when the Empire’s first mountain was split by lightning.
    
- One serpent burrowed deep into the earth, embodying stone, secrecy, and endurance. The other rose into the skies, embodying wind, ambition, and change.
    
- Together, they represent the Empire’s dual nature: rooted in tradition yet ever-reaching outward.
    

### Symbolism

- [[content/Sanao Empire/Green Flame Lanterns]] across the Empire are lit in pairs to honor the Serpents, their twin lights symbolizing balance between below and above.
    
- Contracts, oaths, and treaties are often sealed “in the sight of the Serpents,” binding both the hidden and the revealed.
    
- Masks worn during festivals often depict two serpent heads facing opposite directions, reminding citizens that every choice has a shadow.
    

### Worship & Ritual

- The Serpents are not gods in the traditional sense but primordial forces. Temples are rare; instead, shrines are carved into cliff faces, mines, and marketplaces.
    
- Festivals of the Coil: Held once a year, dancers weave through the streets in paired lines, mimicking the serpents’ endless spiral.
    
- Stone Oaths: Miners and spelunkers place their hands on the rock and invoke the Serpent Below for safety. Merchants light twin lanterns to invoke the Serpent Above for fortune.
    

### Influence in [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Gragos]]

- In [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Gragos]], the Serpents are invoked during trade and intrigue. The market square statue depicts them coiled together, one gazing skyward, the other downward into the earth.
    
- Spies and merchants whisper that the Serpents bless those who can balance secrecy with ambition.
    

### Influence in [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]

- In [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]], miners believe the Serpent Below coils through the caverns, guiding them to veins of ore—or punishing greed with collapse.
    
- Cliffside shrines honor the Serpent Above, asking for safe passage across rope bridges and ridgeways.
    
- The **Trial of the Drop** is said to honor both serpents: trusting the rope (Below) and braving the fall (Above).
    

## Adventure Hooks

- **The Forgotten Coil:** A hidden cult claims the Serpents are not guardians but rivals, and seeks to awaken one against the other.
    
- **Lanterns Extinguished:** Green flames across [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Gragos]] suddenly sputter out—an omen that one Serpent has turned its gaze away.
    
- **The Hollow Choir:** Echoes in [[content/Sanao Empire/Proridge/Proridge]]’s caverns begin to hiss in serpentine voices, hinting that the Serpent Below is stirring.
