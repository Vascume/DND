At the heart of [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Gragos]] lies the Obsidian Court, a vast plaza paved with black stone that gleams like glass under the green lanterns of the Empire. Here, imperial decrees are read aloud by heralds in ash-gray robes, their voices echoing against towering spires. Nobles parade their wealth in silken processions, while spies and assassins weave unseen through the crowd. The Court is both stage and battlefield, where reputation is currency and every word carries the weight of law.
