Large bustling city. 
Population - Roughly 16,000 people

Nestled between jagged cliffs and the silver waters of the Arathis River, **Gragos** is a city that hums with ambition and danger in equal measure. Its skyline bristles with spires of black stone, each carved with the sigils of noble houses that vie for dominance beneath the watchful eye of the [[content/Sanao Empire/Sanao Empire]]. Lanterns of green flame line the streets, casting eerie shadows that dance across the cobblestones long after dusk.

###  Atmosphere

- [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Emberveil Market]]: A cacophony of shouting merchants, exotic spices, and strange beasts brought from distant provinces. The air is thick with incense and the promise of both fortune and betrayal.
    
- [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/The Iron Docks]]: Massive chains creak as barges unload ore, timber, and contraband. Sailors whisper of sea-spirits that linger in the mist, demanding tribute from the unwary.
    
- [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/The Obsidian Court]]: A grand plaza where imperial decrees are read aloud. Here, the nobility parade their wealth, while spies and assassins weave unseen through the crowd.
    

###  Culture & People

- Gragos is a city of **contrasts**: scholars debate philosophy in candlelit halls while gladiators spill blood in the arenas.
    
- The citizens are fiercely loyal to the Empire but equally loyal to their own ambitions. Deals are struck in shadowed taverns, and betrayal is considered an art form.
    
- Festivals honoring the [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Twin Serpents]], ancient guardians said to coil beneath the city, fill the streets with masks, music, and firelight.
    

###  Secrets & Hooks

- Beneath [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Gragos]] lies a labyrinth of tunnels known as the [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Veins]], where smugglers, cultists, and forgotten creatures thrive.
    
- Rumors whisper that the **Sanao Emperor** himself keeps a hidden palace here, shielded from prying eyes by wards older than the Empire.
    
- The city’s ruling council, [[the Circle of Sanur]], is said to be cursed: every member meets a violent end before their term concludes.
