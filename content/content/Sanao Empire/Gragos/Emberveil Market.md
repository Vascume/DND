At the very core of [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Gragos]] lies the [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Emberveil Market]], a sprawling labyrinth of stalls, banners, and stone-arched pavilions that pulse with the lifeblood of the city. The marketplace is never truly silent; even at dawn, the clang of iron bells and the chatter of merchants echo through the streets like a heartbeat.
### Atmosphere

- **Sights**: Brightly dyed fabrics ripple in the wind, exotic beasts snarl from iron cages, and towering braziers burn with green flame to ward off thieves and spirits.
    
- **Sounds**: The cries of hawkers mingle with the rhythmic beat of drummers, while whispers of secret deals drift from shadowed alcoves.
    
- **Smells**: Spiced meats roast on skewers, pungent incense curls through the air, and the sharp tang of freshly hammered steel lingers from the smiths’ corner.
### Key Features

- [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/The Grand Fountain of Serpents]]: A marble basin carved with the [[content/Sanao Empire/Gragos/Twin Serpents]], said to bless trade with prosperity. Coins glitter at its base, offerings from hopeful merchants.
    
- **The Red Awnings**: A district of cloth merchants and jewelers, where silks shimmer like liquid fire and gems are displayed under enchanted lanterns.
    
- **The Shadowed Stalls**: Hidden beneath the arches, these stalls deal in forbidden relics, whispered secrets, and potions that glow faintly in the dark.
